3) The biggest flaws / risks in the idea
❗ 1. You are betting into a parimutuel pool
This is critical.
Your edge:
EV = true_prob – public_prob
But:
when you bet, you MOVE the odds
So scale is capped.
This is very different from:
Betfair exchange
financial markets
→ your own success kills your price.
❗ 2. The take rate is high
JRA take:
~20–30%
So your model must beat:
a ~25% tax
That’s enormous.
Most ML models that “predict winners” are still negative EV after take.
❗ 3. Data access and automation legality
Japan has:
strict terms of service
anti-bot protections
gambling-related compliance issues
Fully automated betting:
may be legally sensitive depending on execution method
So you must separate:
decision system ≠ auto-execution system
❗ 4. Market is not as dumb as it looks
JRA is actually:
one of the most efficient tote markets in the world
The obvious signals are already priced in:
last race speed
jockey
popularity
draw bias
Your edge must come from:
interaction effects
pace shape modelling
latent variables
Not simple ML.
